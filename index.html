The Process

A clear plan for your
email program

We handle strategy, copy, automation setup, and ongoing optimization — so you can focus on your product.

Step 01

Brand Audit

We audit your current email presence, list health, platform setup, and revenue gaps. Your review identifies opportunities; results depend on your business, list and execution.

Step 02

Strategy & Copy

We build your full email calendar — welcome flows, abandoned cart sequences, post-purchase campaigns, and promos — all written for your brand voice.

Step 03

Klaviyo Setup

We configure your automations, segment your list, and set up A/B tests. Every flow is dialed in before it goes live.

Step 04

Ongoing Management

Monthly campaigns, performance reports, and continuous optimization. You see the revenue numbers; we handle the execution.
